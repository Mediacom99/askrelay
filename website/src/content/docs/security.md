title: Security & threat model
description: What the relay sees and doesn't, the approval model, revocation, and responsible disclosure.
---

askrelay's security posture: authenticated-but-never-benign inbound handling, client-side secret redaction, ephemeral retention, immediate device revocation, single-audience short-lived tokens, and the coordinated-disclosure process (see also the repository's SECURITY.md).
