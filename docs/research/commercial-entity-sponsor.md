# Commercialization research — Entity, employer-IP, and sponsor structures for a maintainer-owned OSS project (askrelay) with commercial options — including what actually locks at first external DCO contribution

> Input to the commercialization-posture decision (D-13/D-08 review), researched
> 2026-07-19 against live sources; every evidence item independently re-checked.

## TL;DR

Precedent strongly favors the maintainer's prior: personal ownership plus employer-as-sponsor (curl) and a later personal LLC selling closed add-ons around a permissive core (Sidekiq, Caddy) are the proven solo paths, while transferring IP/trademark to the sponsor (Redis) or splitting mark ownership with exclusive commercial rights (WordPress/Automattic) are the documented disasters. Critically, Apache-2.0 + DCO does NOT lock out open-core or even forward relicensing — Element relicensed Apache-2.0 Synapse to AGPLv3 in 2023 without contributor consent because permissive contributions can be incorporated into any derivative work — what locks at first external contribution is exclusivity (published code stays permissively available to everyone, including hosted competitors, forever) and the social cost of later restriction (Caddy 2017 backlash/fork). The Italian default (art. 12-bis L.633/1941) gives the employer software created "in the performance of duties or on instructions," which is exactly the grey zone askrelay sits in as Kosmoy is the first user — a written employer IP acknowledgment letter (FSF-disclaimer/BEIPA-style) before launch is the single highest-value legal action.

   - Source: <https://curl.se/docs/copyright.html>
8. 🟢 **[primary, statute; verified 2026-07-19]** Italy, art. 12-bis L. 633/1941: 'salvo patto contrario' (unless otherwise agreed), the employer exclusively owns economic exploitation rights in software created by an employee 'in the execution of his duties or on instructions given by the employer'. The statute is silent on out-of-scope/off-hours work (default: stays with employee), but the rule is contractually variable in either direction — the employment contract's IP clause, not the statute, is dispositive.
   - Source: <https://www.brocardi.it/legge-diritto-autore/titolo-i/capo-iii/sezione-i/art12bis.html>
9. 🟡 **[secondary, decision 2020; verified 2026-07-19]** Court of Bologna decision no. 96/2020: software created even by a self-employed developer was held to belong to the commissioning company — Italian courts read employer/client ownership broadly, so 'employer is first user and asked for the tool' is a genuine contamination risk under the 'instructions' prong of 12-bis, not a theoretical one.
   - Source: <https://www.martinimanna.com/blog/court-of-bologna-decision-no-96-2020-the-software-developed-by-the-self-employed-belongs-to-the-employer>

   - Source: <https://github.com/github/balanced-employee-ip-agreement>
11. 🟢 **[primary, current maintainer docs; verified 2026-07-19]** FSF employer copyright disclaimer: decades-established practice and template — for contributors whose employer may have claims, the FSF requires 'a signed piece of paper from your employer disclaiming rights to the program', signed by a VP/GM or anyone authorized to license the company's software. This is the ready-made checklist artifact for both (a) Edoardo vs Kosmoy and (b) Kosmoy colleagues contributing to askrelay, whose contributions are Kosmoy's by default under 12-bis.
   - Source: <https://www.gnu.org/prep/maintain/html_node/Copyright-Papers.html>

   - Source: <https://fossmarks.org/post/4ownership/>

   - Source: <https://cra.mr/sentry-from-the-beginning/>

   - Source: <https://sfconservancy.org/blog/2021/jun/30/who-should-own-foss-copyrights/>

## Assessment

The maintainer's prior survives testing, with one correction and one urgent addition. Correction: the timing fear is miscalibrated. Because Apache-2.0 is permissive, the first external DCO contribution does NOT lock out open-core, a paid hosted tier, or even a forward relicense to AGPL/BSL — Element demonstrated this live in 2023, incorporating no-CLA Apache-2.0 contributions into an AGPLv3 successor. What locks, permanently and in one direction, is exclusivity: every published line stays Apache-2.0 for everyone (including a future hosted competitor) forever, and the 'no CLA ever' promise makes a later dual-licensing business require either a from-now-on CLA (Element's move, with friction) or clean-room separation. The proven solo structure is exactly Sidekiq/Caddy: Apache core + separately-authored closed or hosted-only components, personal entity formed only at commercialization. Urgent addition: the real lock-in risk is not licensing but employer IP and the mark. Italian art. 12-bis hands the employer software made 'on instructions,' Bologna 96/2020 shows courts read this broadly, and Kosmoy being first user plus prospective founding sponsor puts askrelay squarely in the grey zone — Redis is the documented worst case of a founding sponsor absorbing a maintainer's project name and IP. Sponsorship credit itself is safe (Caddy/ZeroSSL); sponsor ownership of mark, org, or domain is not. Failed/boring base rate: most such projects never commercialize at all, and the structures above cost little if that happens.

## Implications for askrelay

Keep D-13 (Apache-2.0 + DCO, no CLA) and D-08 (free hosted demo); they are compatible with a later paid hosted tier without any relicense, because hosted-ops/billing code never needs to be in the OSS repo, and any future paid add-ons can follow the Sidekiq pattern (separate repo, maintainer-only authorship). Do four cheap things before the first external DCO contribution: (1) obtain a written IP acknowledgment letter from Kosmoy, naming askrelay, using FSF-disclaimer or BEIPA-2.0 language (employee owns; employer gets non-exclusive use license) — this is the must-ask for an Italian lawyer, together with: exact wording of the employment contract's IP clause ('patto contrario' can cut either way), whether work done at Kosmoy's request/hours triggers the 'instructions' prong of 12-bis, and whether a paid service later conflicts with art. 2105 duty of loyalty; (2) decide and document now that the trademark owner is Edoardo personally (filing can stay deferred per D-17, ownership decision cannot) and keep org/domain out of Kosmoy's hands, reinforcing D-14; (3) get lightweight employer disclaimers for Kosmoy colleagues' contributions, since 12-bis makes their patches Kosmoy's by default and their DCO sign-offs may otherwise be hollow; (4) pre-commit in the governance doc that the relay core stays Apache-2.0 — pre-registration is the cheapest insurance against Caddy-2017-style backlash if a paid tier ships. Form no entity yet; incorporate (SRL or ditta individuale) only when revenue is imminent.
