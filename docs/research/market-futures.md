# Market research — Futures: adjacencies for askrelay if the core niche (cross-person AI-session messaging) shifts — agent mesh, HITL approval infra, cross-org federation, audit/compliance, A2A bridge

> Market go/no-go input, researched 2026-07-16 against live sources;
> every evidence item independently re-checked (verdicts below).

## TL;DR

The approval-gateway adjacency (b) is the strongest hedge: it is askrelay's existing core (gates + inbox + signed envelopes carry over nearly intact), it has real if early demand signals (YC-backed HumanLayer, gotoHuman, Permit.io, Cloudflare shipping HITL as a platform primitive, 48% of production agents reportedly ungoverned), and it is the one adjacency a vendor's first-party session messaging does NOT cover. Org-internal agent mesh (a) and A2A gateway (e) show the most demand but are already consolidating around foundation-backed, big-vendor-sponsored OSS (agentgateway, Solace Agent Mesh, A2A at 150+ orgs), leaving little room for a solo project. The audit/compliance adjacency (d) lost its 2026 regulatory forcing function: the Digital Omnibus, finally approved June 2026, defers Annex III high-risk obligations (including Article 12 logging) to December 2027, and agent-to-agent comms logging is not an explicit distinct requirement. Cross-org federation (c) fits askrelay's trust-boundary design structurally but demand evidence is still pilots-and-protocols, not purchase orders.

## Assessment

Ranked by (demand evidence x design carryover x vendor-clock insulation): (b) approval gateway wins. Demand is real but early — HumanLayer, gotoHuman, Permit.io are alive and selling, Cloudflare made HITL a documented platform primitive, and the strongest quantitative signal (48% of production agents ungoverned, Gravitee, Apr 2026) is secondary-quality. Crucially, askrelay's v1 carries over almost unchanged: gates-in-both-directions, revocable grants, signed envelopes, and a durable inbox ARE an approval gateway; generalizing from "approve a message" to "approve an action" is an extension, not a rewrite. (a) agent mesh and (e) A2A bridge have the most demand but the worst competitive geometry: agentgateway (LF, backed by AWS/Microsoft/IBM/Cisco), Solace Agent Mesh Enterprise (GA Dec 2025), and A2A itself (150+ orgs) already occupy the gateway/mesh slot; a solo-maintainer relay adds nothing there except A2A conformance, which askrelay should keep as cheap insurance. (c) cross-org federation fits askrelay's design best structurally (self-hosted relay at the trust boundary, per-device signatures, human gates) but demand is still protocols-and-pilots. (d) audit/compliance took a concrete hit: the Digital Omnibus (final June 29, 2026) pushed Annex III logging/oversight obligations to Dec 2027, agent-comms logging is not an explicit requirement, incumbents (Langfuse, LangSmith, Credo AI) are positioned, and askrelay's ephemeral-retention principle is the opposite of an audit layer. Uncertainty: HITL-as-standalone-product may commoditize into platform features before a solo OSS project can matter.

## Implications for askrelay

The vendor-clock threat is partially materializing sideways: Anthropic's Agent Teams (Feb 2026) does same-user session messaging and Claude Tag (June 2026) does shared-channel team AI — neither does cross-person, cross-vendor, approval-gated session messaging yet, but the direction is unmistakable. The adjacency that most de-risks this is (b), the approval gateway, for a structural reason: when a vendor ships first-party cross-person messaging, it will ship it with its own UX and without neutral, self-hosted, policy-controlled gates spanning vendors — the approval layer is exactly the part a vendor is least incentivized to make vendor-neutral. Practical implications for v1: (1) keep "approval-as-the-product" the architectural center, and design the gate/grant/inbox module so an "approvable" is a typed payload of which a message is merely the first kind — that single abstraction is the pivot hinge; (2) keep the envelope A2A-aligned (as decided in D-log) so adjacency (e)/(c) stays open at near-zero cost, but do not compete with agentgateway on gateway features; (3) drop any plan premised on an August 2026 EU compliance forcing function — it moved to December 2027; (4) do not pursue (d) without revisiting ephemeral retention, which currently contradicts it. No rewrite is required for the best hedge; that is the good news of this dimension.
